Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address and a password. As you use Spendly, we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to run Spendly: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to produce the summaries and filters you ask for. We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and kept for as long as your account is active. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. You can delete your expenses or your account at any time, and the associated data will be removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. Beyond this, we do not share your personal data with third parties, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data deleted, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
